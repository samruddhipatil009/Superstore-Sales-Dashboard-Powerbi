### Key Business Insights:
1. **Overall Performance:** $1.6M total sales and $175K profit from 22K orders
2. **Regional Leader:** West Region contributes 33% of sales, with California as top performing state
3. **Customer Segment:** Consumer segment drives 48% of sales, followed by Corporate 33%
4. **Category Performance:** Office Supplies $0.64M, Technology $0.47M, Furniture $0.45M
5. **Top SubCategories:** Phones $0.20M, Chairs $0.18M, Binders $0.17M lead sales
6. **Payment Preference:** 43% customers use COD, 35% prefer Online payment
7. **Shipping Choice:** Standard Class used for $0.33M sales
8. **Seasonal Trend:** Sales peak from September to November yearly
9. **State-wise Analysis:** California, New York, and Texas are top 3 states
10. **Sales Forecast:** 15-day forecast shows positive growth trend for Jan 2021
